# portent-kid-breakdown

- all information gets a label 
  - it's own 'typing' - notes, people, projects, topics, etc
- entities are connected to other entities
  - portent methold - lines are drawn between enteties that go together AKA a 'relationship' 
    - projects belong to: a person / notes are related to: topics
- the lifecycle - progression from captured, to active, to archived 
  - consists of: 
    1. inbox - recent additions not yet explored
    2. play box - entries currently and frequently used
    3. storage box - options for later consideration after continuous use
- In conclusion
  - the whole thing consists of - 
    1. label it: what kind of thing is it?
    2. connect it: what does it go with?
    3. put it in the right box: new/in use/done
- The idea is this: avoid sorting everything into one hoping to remember where it all is later. Instead, do this: label them, connect them, and then follow one to the other regardless of location. 
  - Folders = Shelves = each item can only be on one shelf
  - Labels/connections = better = one item can be two things in one plus a person favorite.
